# Scope, sourcing and limits

This applies to every page in this portfolio.

## What this work is

A personal security operations lab on equipment I own, at my own premises, plus the tooling
and writeups built around it. Everything here was designed, built, broken, measured and
corrected by me, on my own time.

## What it is not

- **Not employer work.** No system, dataset, finding, document or configuration belonging to
  any employer, past or present, appears here. Nothing here is written on any employer's
  behalf and nothing here represents any employer's views, posture or practices.
- **Not client work.** No client data, client environment, engagement deliverable or
  engagement finding appears here. Where a page describes document-generation or assessment
  tooling, it was built and exercised against scenarios and templates of my own making.
- **Not a product, and not advice.** These pages describe what I built and what I measured
  on one estate. They are not a recommendation for your environment, not a specification to
  copy, and not a warranty that any technique, threshold or configuration described will work
  elsewhere or produce the same result. Security controls are context-dependent; calibration
  that is correct here can be wrong somewhere else.

## How to read the numbers

Every figure is a **reading taken on a stated date**, not a permanent property. Volumes,
counts, index sizes, scores and rule totals on a live system move daily, and several figures
on these pages have already moved since they were written. Where a page says *read live*, it
means exactly that and gives the date. Where a number is a vendor rating, a datasheet value
or a console setting rather than something I measured, the page says so.

Where something could not be verified, the page says it could not be verified rather than
filling the gap. Where I previously published something that turned out to be wrong, the
correction is on the page, in place, with the original claim named.

## Sanitisation

Hostnames, user accounts, internal addressing, network segment identifiers, ports, internal
file paths, credentials and device names are deliberately omitted or generalised throughout,
including inside screenshots. Tools and techniques are named because that is the substance;
identifiers are not, because they are not.

**Descriptions of current security configuration are deliberately incomplete.** Where a page
discusses a control's design, rollout order or intent, it is not a statement of the live
configuration of any running service, and should not be read as one.

## Third parties

Product and project names belong to their owners and appear here for identification only. No
affiliation, sponsorship or endorsement is implied in either direction. Where a page reports
that I could not get a particular result out of a third-party tool or API, that is a record
of my own observation and reading of it at that time, not an assertion about the product's
correctness.
